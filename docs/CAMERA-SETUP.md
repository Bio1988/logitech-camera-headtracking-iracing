# Camera setup

- Mount the webcam near the horizontal center of the display.
- Keep the face well lit and avoid strong backlight.
- C920/C920e: start at 640x480 or 720p, 30 FPS.
- C922/Brio/StreamCam: start at 720p60 if stable.
- In OpenTrack choose `NeuralNet Tracker` and select the Logitech camera explicitly.
- Keep MJPEG enabled where supported.
- Bind a Center shortcut and calibrate from your natural driving position.
